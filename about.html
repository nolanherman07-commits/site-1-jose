About me

Residential, commercial, investment and custom builds, all with one agent.

Jose Lugo is a distinguished real estate professional with a reputation for delivering exceptional results across both residential and commercial real estate. In just over three years, Jose has completed more than 115 successful transactions, establishing himself as a go-to expert for clients seeking a seamless experience and unparalleled market insight. From securing dream homes for buyers to achieving top-dollar sales for sellers, Jose consistently demonstrates a commitment to excellence that sets him apart among elite agents worldwide.

In the commercial real estate arena, Jose's expertise shines in complex sales and lease negotiations, guiding business owners and investors to opportunities that align with their strategic goals. His comprehensive knowledge of market trends, property valuation, and investment strategies ensures his clients receive tailored solutions, whether they're looking to expand, diversify, or optimize their portfolios.

A visionary in real estate investment, Jose specializes in property flipping through the BRRRR (Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat) method, leveraging his experience and insight to maximize returns for clients. This approach, popularized by industry leaders, has allowed him to build a reputation for generating significant gains for investors seeking to grow their portfolios.

Beyond residential and commercial transactions, Jose also offers custom home-building services, helping clients bring their dream homes to life on their own lots or through all-inclusive packages. His ability to deliver in such a wide range of services, from luxury residential sales to high-stakes commercial deals and custom builds, cements Jose's position as a trusted advisor for discerning clients who value expertise, innovation, and unmatched commitment to their real estate goals.
