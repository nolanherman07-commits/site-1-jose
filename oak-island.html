Why people love Oak Island

Oak Island is a barrier island town in Brunswick County with a long south-facing beach, two piers and the Intracoastal Waterway behind it. It's a favorite for second homes and short-term rentals as well as full-time island living.

  • Miles of wide, family-friendly beach
  • Oceanfront, second-row and waterway homes
  • Strong short-term rental market
  • Minutes from Southport
